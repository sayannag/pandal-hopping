Durga Puja 2026 · Kolkata

The days & the Metro পাঁজি

Shashthi tithi spans two days this year, so the big nights are Saturday 17 to Tuesday 20 October. Blue and Green Lines run through the night on those four nights.

Field notes

Survive (and enjoy) the crowds টিপস

Kolkata gets tens of lakhs of visitors on Ashtami night. A little strategy turns a queue marathon into a great night.

🌅

Beat the queues

Mega pandals like Sreebhumi, Santosh Mitra Square and Suruchi are calmest before noon and after 2 am. Panchami and Shashthi afternoons are gold.

🚇

Ride the all-night Metro

Blue and Green Lines run through the night 17–20 Oct. The Orange Line is suspended 17–21 Oct. The planner already knows this.

👟

Dress for 15,000 steps

Comfortable shoes, light cotton, a power bank, water and small cash for phuchka. Barricades and one-way flows can double walking distance.

🍛

Eat off-peak

Arsalan, Peter Cat and Golbari see long waits after 8 pm. Eat around 6 pm or snack on rolls and phuchka between pandals.

📶

Save it offline

Networks jam in crowds. Once loaded, this site keeps working offline — optimise your route at home and screenshot or print the timeline.

🧭

Pick a meeting point

Agree on a landmark — a Metro gate or a sweet shop — in case your group gets separated in the crush.

🪔

Don't skip the bonedi baris

Family pujas like Shobhabazar Rajbari (since 1757) and Sabarna Roy Choudhury (since 1610) are calm, old and beautiful.

🆘

Emergencies

Dial 112 for any emergency, 100 for police, 101 for fire. Kolkata Police help kiosks sit near most big pandals.

Ratings, straight from Google

How Google ratings work here

Works out of the box

Every food spot has a ★ Google rating button that opens Google's own place card — live rating, review count, photos and hours. Every pandal page also has a live Google food map showing every restaurant around it. Nothing is cached or copied, so ratings are always current.

Want ratings inside the lists?

Connect your own Google Maps Platform key to see ratings, review counts, price level and opening status directly in every list — and let the planner choose food stops by rating.

  1. In Google Cloud Console, enable Maps JavaScript API and Places API (New).
  2. Create an API key and restrict it to your site (e.g. https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/*).
  3. Paste it here — it's stored only in this browser. Site owners can also set it in config.js.

What you get with a key

  • Top-rated places within walking distance of every pandal, live from Google.
  • Rating ★, number of reviews, price level and "open now".
  • Official website and Google Maps listing (with menu photos) for each place.
  • Food stops in your route picked by rating, cravings and detour time.

Google's free monthly usage is plenty for personal planning. Set a daily quota on the key if you publish the site. Results are shown with Google attribution and are not stored.
